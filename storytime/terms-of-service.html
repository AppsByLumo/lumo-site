How Your Data is Used

We aim to use your data responsibly and protect your privacy.

We use artificial intelligence (AI) to create your stories, audiobooks, and heroes using models provided by companies like OpenAI, Google, ElevenLabs, and xAI.

When you use these features, the app securely sends your request to Lumo's servers first. From there, we send the minimum needed to each AI model that creates your story, audiobook, or hero. That request can include things like:

  • The hero you picked or photo you took to create a story
  • The story you're turning into an audiobook
  • The reader's age, reading level, and language
  • The reader's name and gender, if "Include Me in the Story" is turned on
  • Details you enter when creating a custom hero, like appearance, skills, and description

This information is used to create your story and match the reader's vocabulary, pacing, and themes.

Lumo's servers keep a copy of the stories and audiobooks we create for you. We use these copies to help with support — for example, looking up a story if something unexpected came up. We may also re-use highly rated stories in the future with other readers. These copies are removed when you delete your Lumo account.

Lumo also collects anonymous usage information through Google Analytics to help us understand how often the app is used, which features are used most, and whether things are working well.

  • "Share How You Use StoryTime" is on by default
  • You can turn off this setting at any time
  • We use a unique but anonymous ID to understand how people use the app over time
  • This anonymous ID is reset once per year
  • If you turn off "Share How You Use StoryTime" and back on again, you will have a new anonymous ID

We never use your data for ads, and we never sell it to anyone.

Magic Camera

Magic Camera lets you take a photo and use it to inspire a story. When you use it:

  • Your photo is sent through Lumo's servers to an AI model so it can inspire the details of your story
  • The photo is saved alongside the story it helped create, on your device
  • It is not added to your Photos app
  • Lumo's servers don't keep a copy of your photo. They may keep a short text description the AI wrote of what's in the photo, so the story can use it later.
  • If you turn on Lumo Cloud, your story and its photo are uploaded to your Lumo account so you can use them on your other devices

You can turn off Magic Camera at any time in Settings.

Only upload photos that are yours to use. If other people are in the photo, make sure they're okay with being part of your story. Please don't upload anything copyrighted, inappropriate, or harmful.

Accounts, Backups, and Resetting

To use Lumo StoryTime, you'll create a free Lumo account using Sign in with Apple. Your Lumo account keeps your Stars safe across devices.

Lumo Cloud is included with Star Pass. It saves a backup of your stories, heroes, photos, and settings, and lets you sync them between your devices.

Resetting:

  • "Reset All Stories & Settings" deletes your on-device library and resets the app, including the device ID we use to help protect your account — it's not used for tracking or ads
  • Turning off Lumo Cloud stops future backups and syncing, but doesn't automatically delete data already saved in Lumo Cloud
  • Deleting a story, audiobook, or hero from Lumo Cloud also removes it from your other devices
  • You can choose to delete your Lumo account at any time — this removes your account, your Stars, your Lumo Cloud, and the server-side copies of every story and audiobook you've created

Stars: Your Stars are kept safe on Lumo's servers and tied to your Lumo account, so they stay with you on any device you sign in on.
